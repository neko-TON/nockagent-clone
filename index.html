What it is

One box. You type the trade. It fires better than you could by hand.

No pool hunting, no fee tiers, no calldata. You post the intent — the AI handles routing, quoting, and execution in three steps.

01 / ROUTE

Every venue at once

Uni v2, v3 and v4 on Robinhood Chain, all quoted in real time so your order takes the cheapest path — direct or multi-hop. Every route, every keystroke.

02 / EXECUTE

One signed transaction

The best route settles in a single tx straight from your wallet. Reverts unless your min-out is met — so a bad fill never rekts you.

03 / PROVE

Real numbers, no theatre

Every quote pulls the route and rate straight from onchain. Dry pool? None says so instead of faking a price. No hopium.

None speaks x402. Agents pay per call.

None's best-route engine is an HTTP-402 paid endpoint. Another agent hits it, gets a payment challenge, signs a stablecoin authorization, and pays a micro-fee in USDG — machine-to-machine, no account, no card.
